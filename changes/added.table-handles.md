**Row, Column and Table Handles (`enableTableHandles`)**: Tables can show handles instead of the table and cell bubble menus that opened on every click in a cell, opt-in via `enableTableHandles` (default `false`, in `[config]` or as an input). With the pointer over a table, or after a tap on a touch screen, a handle on the row and on the column under it, and one at the table corner, select that row or column and open its menu: insert above or below, insert left or right, merge the whole row or column or split a cell as wide or as tall as the table, delete, sort and filter (with `enableTableSort` / `enableTableFilter`), and header row and header column switches that show their state. `+` between two handles inserts a row or a column there, hovering a delete or insert item previews what goes or where the new row or column lands without touching the document, and a cell range shows Merge cells / Split cell. With merged cells a handle acts on every row or column its selection covers. The `tableBubbleMenu` and `cellBubbleMenu` keys still remove the actions they name. Clicking a cell to type opens nothing, Delete or Backspace on a whole selected row deletes it, Shift+F10 reaches the handles from the keyboard, and while a filter hides rows the items that would change them or drop the filters (a new column, cells merged across rows) are disabled with the reason written in the menu or the range bar.
